

### Partitives

Express part of the following noun phrase, or antecedent of following pronoun.

| All  | Some | None |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| tout | ???  | nal  |

Examples
- tout er = all of him
- ??? os = some of it
- nal eis = none of them

### Determiners

| Syntactic Group | Definite           | Proximate                                    | Distal                              | Free-Choice              | Negative                                      | Epistemic                   |
| --------------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Determiners** | y.n                | hai.n                                        | atty.n                              | any.n                    | oudy.n                                        | somy.n                      |
| **Pronouns**    | er...<br>and<br>ou | hair..., haies<br>haind (now)<br>haio (here) | atter...<br>attand<br>attou (there) | aner...<br>anand<br>anou | ouder...<br>oudand (never)<br>oudou (nowhere) | somer...<br>somand<br>somou |

Usage:
- **Definite**: Generic definiteness.  To signify to a listener that they should know from context which referent exactly is being referred to.
- **Proximate**: Like definiteness, but indicating the referent which is closest to the speaker.  Frequently being gestured to physically.
- **Distal**: Like definiteness, but indicating a referent that is far from the speaker.  Frequently being gestured to physically.
- **Free-Choice**: Used to express that the exact referent doesn't matter.  Any referent pulled from the noun set would suffice.
	- e.g. *any book*, *n'importe quel livre*, *qualquier libro*
- **Epistemic**: Used to express a specific unknown referent.
	- e.g. *some book*, *algún libro*, *quelque livre* (*je ne sais quel livre*)
