Definitions
- royal
	- (monarchy) Of, relating to, or belonging to a crown or a monarch; regal.
	- (by extension, especially in law) Of or belonging to the Crown; of the state or the government.

Etymology
- [coron](../Nouns/coron.md) + [-ar](../Adjectival%20Suffixes/-ar.md)
