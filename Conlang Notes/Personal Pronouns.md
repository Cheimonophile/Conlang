Used like personal pronouns in other languages

| Person / Number   | Nominative | Accusative | Disjunctive | Possessive Determiner | Possessive Adjective |
| ----------------- | ---------- | ---------- | ----------- | --------------------- | -------------------- |
| **1st Singular**  | ya         | me         | my          | my/n                  | mò                   |
| **2nd Singluar**  | tu         | te         | ty          | ty/n                  | tò                   |
| **1st Plural**    | einnar     | einnam     | einnam      | einny/n               | einnò                |
| **2nd Plural**    | oussar     | oussam     | oussam      | oussy/n               | oussò                |
| **3rd Animate**   | ér         | ém         | éy          | eny/n                 | enò                  |
| **3rd Inanimate** | ós         | ós         | ós          | ony/n                 | onò                  |
| **3rd Plural**    | anes       | anes       | anes        | ansy/n                | ansò                 |


Examples:
- ya loves te = I love you
- ya loves myn amec = I love my friend
