  - (habitual auxiliary)

Definitions
- (auxiliary) forms the [habitual tenses](../../Verbs.md#Habitual) with a present participle

Properties
- Auxiliary

Irregular Forms
- Present: fùs, fùt, fùnt
- Past: fou, fùno
- Future: fùre, fùro, fùren
- Present Participle: fuient
- Past Participle: fum

Examples
- fùt attrahient = it attracts
