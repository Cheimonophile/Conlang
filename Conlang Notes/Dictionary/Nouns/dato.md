Definitions
- date
	- a full calendar day, with day and night

Etymology
- (General European)
	- date (French)
	- Datum (German)
	- дата (Russian)
	- date (English)

Notes
- Retains the Latin theme vowel because this word was a learned borrowing from latin in all of the languages that have it.  It didn't evolve naturally in any of them.
- Preferred -o from Latin *datum* to distinguish other words of similar etymology, such as uncountable *data* which comes from Latin neuter plural.
