Definitions
- frontier
	- the edge of settled or civilized territory, facing an unsettled region, another country or wilderness

Etymology
- ad ultra (Latin)

Notes
- A deliberate borrowing, built from the Latin words *ad* "to" and *ultra* "beyond". *Ad ultra* is not attested as a Latin phrase.
