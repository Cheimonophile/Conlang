Translations:
  - (habitual auxiliary)
---

Definitions
- (auxiliary) forms the [habitual tenses](../../Verbs.md#Habitual) with an infinitive

Properties
- Auxiliary

Irregular Forms
- Conjugates like [voli](voli.md)

Etymology
- soleō (Latin)
- soler (Spanish)

Notes
- Takes the stem *sol-* and the irregular conjugation of [voli](voli.md) by analogy, rather than the *sole-* stem the Romance borrowing steps give from *soleō*.

Examples
- sout attrahi = it attracts
