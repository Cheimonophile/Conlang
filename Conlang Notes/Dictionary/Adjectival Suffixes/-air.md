Definitions
- *Forms adjectives of relationship in words borrowed from Romance.*

Usage
- Not productive.  *-air* appears only in borrowed adjectives such as [reyair](../Adjectives/reyair.md).  New relational meanings use [adjunct nouns](../../Nouns.md#Adjunct%20Nouns), e.g. *card baņces*.

Syncretism
- -ālis (Latin)
- -āris (Latin)
- -ēlis (Latin)
