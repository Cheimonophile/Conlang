Definitions
- frontier
	- the edge of settled or civilized territory, facing an unsettled region, another country or wilderness

Etymology
- (Wanderwort)
	- granica (Polish)
	- Grenze (German)

Notes
- Shaped to mirror how *granica* became German *Grenze*, where the *a* was umlauted to *e* by the *i* of the next syllable and the final vowel was dropped (Middle High German *greniz*). Here the final syllable loses its back vowel, and the *i* moves back into the first syllable, lengthening it to *ai*: *granica* > *granic* > *grainc*.
- The Slavic *c* [ts] is borrowed as *tz*: *grainc* > *graintz*.
