Definitions
- turf
	- live grass together with the dirt its roots hold; grass-covered ground
	- refers to the turf as a whole, not to grass as a plant

Etymology
- gazon (French)
	- borrowed directly from French without the Romance strategy's steps, since French gazon descends from Frankish *wasō "sod, turf" and has no Latin etymon
